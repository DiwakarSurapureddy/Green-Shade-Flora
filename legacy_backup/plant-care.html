Plant Description & Care

Proper plant care ensures healthy growth, longevity, and environmental balance. Below is an example of how to care for a common medicinal plant.

Tulsi (Holy Basil)

Tulsi is a sacred medicinal plant widely used in Indian households. It is known for its immunity-boosting, antibacterial, and stress-relief properties.

🌞 Sunlight

Requires at least 4–6 hours of direct sunlight daily. Best grown in a sunny outdoor location or near a window.

💧 Water

Water the plant regularly but avoid overwatering. Soil should remain slightly moist, not soggy.

🌱 Soil

Well-drained, nutrient-rich soil with good aeration helps in healthy root development.

🛠 Maintenance Tips

  • Prune regularly to encourage bushy growth
  • Remove dry or damaged leaves
  • Avoid chemical pesticides
  • Use organic compost once a month
