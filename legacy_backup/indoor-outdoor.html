Indoor vs Outdoor Plants

Plants grow differently based on their environment. The comparison below highlights key differences between indoor and outdoor plants.

Feature Indoor Plants Outdoor Plants
Light Requirement Indirect or low sunlight Direct sunlight
Space Limited space (pots) Large open area
Maintenance Low to medium Medium to high
Watering Less frequent More frequent
Examples Snake Plant, Money Plant Rose, Sunflower

Indoor Plants

Indoor plants enhance air quality, improve mood, and are ideal for apartments and offices.

  • Require less sunlight
  • Grow well in pots
  • Low maintenance

Outdoor Plants

Outdoor plants grow naturally under sunlight and contribute significantly to the ecosystem.

  • Need direct sunlight
  • Require more space
  • Seasonal maintenance
