Types of Plants

Plants are classified into different categories based on their growth, structure, and uses. Understanding these types helps us choose and care for plants better.

Flowering Plants

Flowering plants produce flowers and seeds. They are widely grown for beauty, fragrance, and decoration in gardens and homes.

Examples: Rose, Sunflower, Jasmine

Non-Flowering Plants

These plants do not produce flowers. They reproduce through spores and usually grow in moist or shaded environments.

Examples: Ferns, Mosses

Medicinal Plants

Medicinal plants are used in traditional and modern medicine to treat various health problems naturally.

Examples: Tulsi, Aloe Vera, Neem

Aquatic Plants

Aquatic plants grow in water bodies like ponds, lakes, and rivers. They help maintain water ecosystems.

Examples: Lotus, Water Hyacinth

Shrubs

Shrubs are medium-sized woody plants with multiple stems, commonly used in landscaping.

Examples: Hibiscus, Cotton
